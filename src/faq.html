Frequently Asked Questions

What is Metajurassic?

Metajurassic is a daily guessing game where you try to identify a mystery dinosaur species. Each guess reveals taxonomic relationships on a phylogenetic tree, helping you narrow down the target.

How do I play?

Type the name of a dinosaur species into the input box and press Enter. The tree will update to show how your guess relates to the mystery species. Use the clades revealed in the tree to guide your next guess.

What does the tree show?

The tree displays the phylogenetic (evolutionary) relationships between your guesses and the mystery species. Clades are shared ancestor groups. The closer your guess is to the ? node on the tree, the closer you are to the answer. A guessed node's colour says the same thing, running cold to hot with the brightest green closest, on the same steps as the squares in the grid you share at the end of a round.

What is the info panel?

Clicking any node in the tree opens a panel with details about that species or clade. After each guess it holds the clade directly above the ? node - the narrowest group the answer is known to belong to. On a wide screen the panel opens beside the tree on its own; on a phone it would cover the tree, so it waits behind the labelled tab at the top right, which names the clade it is holding.

I am stuck. Can I get a hint?

Yes, for a price. A hint reveals the shallowest unrevealed clade in the answer's lineage that meaningfully cuts the remaining field, so it narrows the field without ever naming the answer. That makes it a way out of a round you were going to lose, not a shortcut to a quick win.

What is the round summary?

When a round ends, the summary lists the clades your guesses established, root-first, each one next to the guesses that revealed it. It is a read of the tree you already played, so it stops at the deepest clade you reached - it never tells you how much further down the answer was.

Is there a new puzzle every day?

Yes! A new mystery species is selected each day. The puzzle rolls over at your local midnight, and the end-of-round screen counts down to it.

Can I play more than once a day?

Practice mode deals a random dinosaur as often as you like, and it is kept apart from the daily puzzle everywhere - its own saved rounds, its own stats, its own share text. Adding ?seed=42 to the practice page loads a chosen, reproducible round; seeds are folded into seed mod 100000, so ?seed=100042 is the same round as ?seed=42. Practice rounds resume, seeded ones included, so returning to a seed you have played brings that round back. Press New game to deal a fresh, unseeded one.

How does sharing work?

The share text is one square per guess, in the order you made them, coloured by how close that guess landed - the same cold-to-hot steps the tree uses. A bulb marks each hint you bought. Practice rounds are labelled as practice, so a share never passes one off as the daily.

What does the profile page track?

Games played, win rate, current and longest streak, average guesses, the distribution of your winning guess counts, how much of the collection you have discovered, and a rolling average of your recent rounds. Daily and practice are counted separately, on their own tabs, so practice cannot inflate a daily streak.

What is in the archives?

The species archive is every dinosaur the game can pick, each with its own page. The clades archive is the taxonomic groups those species are sorted into - the same groups the tree reveals as you guess. Both are browsable at any time and give nothing away about today's answer.

Where can I read more about how this works?

The documentation site goes further than this page: the hint rule, practice seeds and what they resume, what the profile counts, how the dinosaur content is authored, and how the repository is put together.
